# EMTAF v1.9 Role Training Facilitator Guide

## Delivery model
1. All-staff security/basic module.
2. Role-specific module.
3. Hands-on demo using the EMTAF wireframe/sandbox.
4. Scenario exercise.
5. Knowledge check.
6. Supervisor sign-off.

## Core teaching rules
- Always confirm tenant and record context.
- Never share credentials.
- Use least privilege.
- Do not bypass a permission restriction.
- Do not repeatedly retry ambiguous critical transactions.
- Preserve auditability.
- Escalate privacy, security, financial, clinical, academic or data-integrity incidents.

## Suggested evidence of competency
- User completes three normal workflows without assistance.
- User correctly handles one failure/exception scenario.
- User explains two role boundaries and two risks.
- User identifies the correct escalation path.

## Training environment
Use non-production/sample data. Do not use real sensitive information for training unless explicitly approved by organizational policy.
